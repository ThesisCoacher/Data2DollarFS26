Technische Anforderungen an den Code:
- Python 3 kompatibel
- Nutze aussagekräftige Variablennamen
- Theme-Setup am Anfang:
  * sns.set_theme(style="whitegrid")  # oder "darkgrid", "white", "dark", "ticks"
  * sns.set_palette("husl")  # oder "Set2", "viridis", "coolwarm"
- Distribution Plots:
  * sns.histplot(data, x=..., hue=..., kde=True, bins=50)
  * sns.violinplot(data, x=..., y=..., hue=..., split=True)
  * sns.boxplot(data, x=..., y=..., palette=...)
- Categorical Plots:
  * sns.countplot(data, x=..., hue=..., order=...)
  * sns.barplot(data, x=..., y=..., estimator=...)
- Correlation & Regression:
  * sns.heatmap(corr_matrix, annot=True, cmap='coolwarm', center=0)
  * sns.regplot(data, x=..., y=..., scatter_kws={...}, line_kws={...})
- Multi-variate:
  * sns.pairplot(data, hue=..., diag_kind='kde')
  * sns.jointplot(data, x=..., y=..., kind='hex'/'scatter'/'reg')
- FacetGrid:
  * g = sns.FacetGrid(data, col=..., hue=..., height=5)
  * g.map(sns.histplot, 'variable')
- Speichern:
  * plt.savefig('seaborn_plot.png', dpi=300, bbox_inches='tight')
